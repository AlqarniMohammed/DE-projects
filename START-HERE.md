# Start Here — the two-minute introduction

*The one page to read (or share) before anything else. No jargon, no prerequisites for reading it.*

## What this is

A free, self-paced training framework for becoming a **data engineer** — the person who builds the systems that move, store, clean, and serve a company's data. You don't collect certificates of completion or watch a course from a couch: you **build one real data platform, piece by piece**, over seven phases, and you keep everything you build as public proof of what you can do.

## Who it's for

- You know **SQL** reasonably well and have touched a cloud console at least once.
- You can give it **~10 focused hours a week** for the 12-month arc — or fewer hours over a longer runway (the pace is yours; see below).
- You want a portfolio that proves skill, not a certificate that claims it.

Not you? If you're further along, there's a [placement guide](reference/LEARNERS.md) for entering mid-way. If you're earlier (no SQL yet), learn SQL basics first — this framework assumes them.

## What you'll have at the end

- A **working data platform** on AWS — ingestion, storage, transformation, orchestration, quality checks, dashboards, and an AI-data pipeline — built by you, explained by you, running for under $25/month.
- **Two respected certifications** (AWS Data Engineer Associate, Databricks Data Engineer Associate) passed along the way.
- A set of **smaller side projects** showing breadth with different tools.
- A written **journey post** — the story of your platform, in the format real engineering teams publish.

## What it costs

AWS at most **$25/month** (protected by a budget alarm you set on day one), **~$310–455 total** for the two exams and a couple of paid courses across the whole journey, and a few dollars of AI-assistant credit for two later steps (or an existing subscription). Everything else is free.

## How it works — the honest version

You move forward by passing **gates**: checklists of things you must have *built and can demo* — a screenshot, a video, a working repo. No gate, no next phase. The calendar never decides; your evidence does. The default arc is 12 months, but a `/study-plan` helper (a Claude Code skill in your fork — or a [four-step recipe](GUIDE.md#pacing-the-year-to-your-life) if you don't use one) fits the same path honestly into your months and hours — and tells you plainly when a timeline doesn't fit.

## Your first hour

1. Read this page — done.
2. Open **[GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md)** and do its [Day 0 setup](GUIDE.md#day-0--set-yourself-up) (~30–45 minutes: fork the repo, read the README and the standing rules, stamp your start date, optionally set your pace).
3. Open [Phase 0](phases/phase-0-orientation/README.md) and follow the loop the guide shows you. Phase 0 walks you through installing every tool — you need nothing prepared.
